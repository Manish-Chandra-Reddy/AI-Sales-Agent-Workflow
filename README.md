# AI-Sales-Agent-Workflow
AI-powered agent to automatically curate and deliver timely, contextual company insights—sourced from financial data, news, and social media to sales representatives before meetings so that they are better informed and reduce manual research time for them


https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/66be8370-7b56-474a-85b9-ef46b03a4df5


Target Segment
• Primary Users: Sales representatives (ages 25–45) in enterprise software, financial services,
and B2B consulting firms.
• Secondary Stakeholders: Sales managers, business development, customer success teams.
• Technical Enablers: AI/IT teams, product managers.
• Strategic Leaders: CRO, CIO, compliance teams.
• Indirect Actors: Clients and data vendors.
• All operate in environments with multiple client meetings each week and rely heavily on
laptops, mobile devices, CRMs, and professional networking platforms for daily work.

Key Pain Points
• Time-intensive preparation: Hours spent manually sourcing insights from scattered
channels.
• Information overload: Difficulty identifying what is most critical and relevant.
• Missed or outdated insights: Rapid market changes make staying current difficult.
• Inconsistent preparation quality: Varies due to individual skills, time, and resources.

What Does Success Look Like?
• Efficiency gains: At least a 50% reduction in time spent on pre-meeting research, validated
through time-tracking and self-reported surveys.
• Stronger preparedness: At least 75% of sales representative’s report feeling “wellprepared” or “very well-prepared” for client meetings within the first 30 days of system use.
• Improved meeting effectiveness: A minimum 20% improvement in client feedback ratings
related to personalization and relevance of meetings, tracked using structured post-meeting
surveys.
• Business impact: A measurable 10–15% uplift in opportunity-to-deal conversion rates
within six months of adoption, validated using CRM analytics.
• User adoption: At least 80% active usage among target sales teams within the first quarter,
demonstrating real integration into daily workflows
